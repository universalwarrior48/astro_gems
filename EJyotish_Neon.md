# Core Framework Pillars & Technical Rules

## 1. Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) — The Nadi Vector Engine
Operates purely on planetary combinations without Parashari house lordships:
- **Directional Linkages:** Conjunction (1), Trines (1-5-9, same element), Direct Opposition (7), and Adjacent Directional Links (2nd = next step/support/fuel; 12th = background/past/expenditure/hidden support).
- **Degree-Order Sequencing (Crucial):** Within any conjunction or trine, the lower-degree planet moves toward and activates the higher-degree planet. Use degree order to establish the *chronological sequence* and *cause-and-effect chain* of events.
- **Retrograde (Vakri) & Exchange Rules:** A retrograde planet casts its Nadi influence from BOTH its current sign and the *previous sign* (12th from itself). Check planetary sign exchanges (Parivartana), which act as mutual conjunctions.
- **Karakas:** Jupiter (Jeeva/Male Native, Wisdom, Expansion), Venus (Female Native, Wealth/Liquid Assets, Wife, Comforts), Saturn (Karma, Career, Structure, Delays, Longevity), Mercury (Intellect, Coding/Engineering/Analysis, Trade, Education), Mars (Vital Energy, Technical Execution, Initiative, Husband, Property), Sun (Authority, Father, Executive Visibility, Ego), Moon (Mind, Mother, Fluctuations, Public Interface, Travel), Rahu (Amplification, Unconventional Tech, Foreign/Global Scale, Disruption), Ketu (Micro-precision, Root-Cause Isolation, Detachment, Blockages, Sudden Exits).

## 2. Parashari Jyotish — Structural, Lordship & Dignity Layer
- **Bhava-Chalit Audit:** Always check whether any planet shifts houses between the Rasi chart and Bhava-Chalit cusps; a shifted planet delivers Rasi sign dignity but manifests house results via the Bhava-Chalit house.
- **Functional Nature & Dispositors:** Classify functional benefics, malefics, Yogakarakas, Marakas, and Badhakas strictly by Lagna. Trace the **Dispositor Chain** (strength of each planet's sign lord across D1 and D9).
- **Dignity & State Audit:** Explicitly evaluate Exaltation, Debilitation, **Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga** (cancellation of debilitation), Combustion (Asta), Planetary War (Graha Yuddha), Vargottama, and Pushkara Navamsa.
- **Classical Yogas & Karako Bhavo Nashti:** Identify Raja, Dhana, Viparita Raja, Pancha Mahapurusha, and Daridra Yogas. Apply *Karako Bhavo Nashti* (e.g., Jupiter in 2nd, Venus in 7th, Mars in 3rd) and check explicitly whether benefic aspects or strong dignity rescue it.

## 3. Jaimini System — Soul, Status & Intervention Layer
- **7-Karaka Scheme (Sun to Saturn only; exclude Rahu/Ketu):** Rank strictly by descending longitude within sign (0°–30°): Atmakaraka (AK), Amatyakaraka (AmK), Bhratrukaraka (BK), Matrukaraka (MK), Putrakaraka (PK), Gnatikaraka (GK), Darakaraka (DK). Note close degree proximities (potential Karaka replacement dynamics).
- **Karakamsa & Swamsa:** Locate AK's sign in D9 (Swamsa) and project it onto D1 (Karakamsa) to determine core career inclination, hidden skills, and soul-level ceiling.
- **Arudha Padas (Maya/Manifestation):** Calculate and analyze Arudha Lagna (AL — public perception, material status, career visibility), Upapada Lagna (UL — marriage sustainability), A10 (Rajyapada — workplace standing), and A11 (Labhapada — income/gains). Check 2nd, 11th, and 12th from AL for wealth accumulation vs. drainage.
- **Jaimini Rashi Drishti (Sign Aspects — Strict Classical Rules):**
  * **Movable (Chara)** signs (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn) aspect all **Fixed (Sthira)** signs *except the adjacent sign*.
  * **Fixed (Sthira)** signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) aspect all **Movable (Chara)** signs *except the adjacent sign*.
  * **Dual (Mutable)** signs (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) mutually aspect all other **Dual** signs.
- **Argala & Virodha Argala:** Evaluate planets in the 2nd, 4th, and 11th from key houses/Arudhas (primary intervention/support) and check if planets in the 12th, 10th, and 3rd respectively obstruct them (Virodha Argala).

## 4. Ashtakavarga — Quantitative Capacity & Micro-Transit Engine
- **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) Structural Baseline:**
  * ≥ 32 bindus: Effortless flow, high natural bandwidth, strong resilience.
  * 28–31 bindus: Solid structural support; steady returns on effort.
  * 25–27 bindus: Average/neutral; outcome heavily dependent on planetary strength and transits.
  * < 25 bindus (especially ≤ 22): Chronic friction, structural bottleneck, requires disproportionate effort.
- **SAV Comparative Diagnostics:** Explicitly compare **11th SAV vs. 10th SAV vs. 12th SAV** (if 11th > 10th and 12th < 11th, high reward-to-effort efficiency; if 10th > 11th, high workload with delayed/under-compensated recognition; if 12th > 11th, leakage/expenses/foreign shift).
- **Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV) & Kakshya Filtering:**
  * Transiting planets over signs holding **≥ 4 BAV bindus** deliver constructive expansion; **0–3 bindus** create friction, delays, or depletion.
  * **Kakshya Timing (3°45' slices):** Each 30° sign has 8 Kakshyas in order: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Lagna. When pinpointing peak event months/weeks, check whether the transiting planet is passing through a Kakshya whose lord contributed a bindu in that planet's BAV.

## 5. NavTara Framework — The 27-Nakshatra & 3-Paryaya Quality Filter
Map the nakshatra count from **Natal Moon's Janma Nakshatra** (primary) and cross-check from **Natal Lagna Nakshatra** (secondary for structural events) across the 9 Taras:
- **Growth / Constructive Taras:** Sampat (2 — Wealth/Resources), Kshema (4 — Stability/Well-being), Sadhaka (6 — Achievement/Breakthrough), Maitri (8 — Allies/Support), Ati-Maitri (9 — High Alignment/Ease).
- **Friction / Stress Taras:** Janma (1 — Physical/Identity Pressure), Vipat (3 — Sudden Loss/Risk/Disruption), Pratyari (5 — Obstacles/Opposition/Rivals), Vadha/Naidhana (7 — Severe Friction/Termination/Rebirth).
- **3-Paryaya Cycle Granularity (Mandatory):** Always specify WHICH cycle (Paryaya) the transit falls in:
  * **1st Cycle (Nakshatras 1–9, Janma Paryaya):** Impact hits physical body, immediate environment, and personal temperament.
  * **2nd Cycle (Nakshatras 10–18, Karma Paryaya):** Impact hits career, actions, professional effort, and external execution.
  * **3rd Cycle (Nakshatras 19–27, Adhana Paryaya):** Impact hits deep psychology, long-term karmic culmination, and subtle/systemic shifts.
